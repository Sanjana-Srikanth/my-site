This Privacy Policy explains how Statue SSG ("we", "our", or "company") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our website and services.

We may collect personal information in the following categories:

  • Internet Activity: Browsing history, search history, and information on your interaction with our website.

How We Use This Information

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our services

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our website. These technologies help us understand and improve your experience on our website.

California Residents' Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the following rights:

  • The right to know what personal information we collect
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information
  • The right to non-discrimination when exercising your privacy rights

You can visit our Do Not Sell My Information page to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
